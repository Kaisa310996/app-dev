# My Favorite Series & Movie (MCU Picks)

Here is a list of my favorite **Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)** series and movie, featuring multiverse chaos, synthezoid identity, and street-level heroism.

---

## 1. VisionQuest *(Series)*
**VisionQuest** follows the rebooted *White Vision* as he goes on the run and communicates with the AI personas embedded in his programming—such as J.A.R.V.I.S., F.R.I.D.A.Y., and Ultron—to understand his identity and reconnect with his son, Tommy.

* **Genre:** Sci-Fi / Superhero / Drama
* **Why I like it:** It serves as a great conclusion to the *WandaVision* trilogy and dives deep into Vision's memories and humanity.

## 2. Loki *(Series)*
**Loki** picks up after the events of *Avengers: Endgame*, following the God of Mischief as he is arrested by the **Time Variance Authority (TVA)** and gets pulled into a massive threat across the multiverse.

* **Genre:** Sci-Fi / Fantasy / Action
* **Highlights:**
  * Incredible character development from villain to guardian of the multiverse
  * Great soundtrack and retro-futuristic TVA aesthetic

> *"Glorious purpose!"* — Loki

## 3. Spider-Man: Brand New Day *(Movie)*
**Spider-Man: Brand New Day** follows Peter Parker living completely on his own after the world forgot his identity, balancing his isolated personal life while protecting New York City against a dangerous new telepathic threat.

* **Genre:** Action / Adventure / Superhero
* **Why I like it:** It brings Spider-Man back to his grounded New York roots while pushing his powers and resilience to the next level.

---

### Top Reasons I Love These Titles
1. Strong character arcs and emotional storytelling
2. High-stakes sci-fi and superhero action
3. Deep connections to the wider Marvel universe
